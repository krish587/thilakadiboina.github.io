## About Me

Hi, I'm Thilak.

I work as an Associate Fellow in AI Science at Verizon, where I focus on applying AI, machine learning, and causal reasoning to solve complex real-world problems.

My journey in data science and analytics spans close to two decades, across telecom, automotive, retail, and other business domains.

Over the years, one thing has become increasingly clear to me. Building a good model is only part of the challenge. Understanding why something happens, whether we can trust the result, and what action we should take is equally important.

This has shaped my interest in Causal AI, Agentic AI, intelligent decision systems, and making AI more reliable.

I'm also pursuing doctoral research in Information Systems and Business Analytics at IIM Ranchi. My research explores economic data quality, anomaly detection, and how unreliable data can influence business decisions and AI systems.

I have co-authored research publications, hold five AI patents, and was recognized among India's 40 Under 40 Data Scientists in 2021.

## Why I Started This Website

I spend considerable time reading AI research papers, exploring new methods, questioning existing approaches, and thinking about how these ideas can be applied to real problems.

Many interesting research papers are difficult to follow, especially for people who use AI but don't necessarily build models.

I wanted a place to share what I learn in simpler language, with examples that make the concepts easier to understand.

Sometimes I'll explain a research paper. Sometimes I'll experiment with a model or a publicly available dataset. Other times, I'll share my observations about AI, analytics, and business decisions.

The objective is to make the learning useful for both technical and nontechnical readers.

## What Interests Me

**Agentic AI and AI systems**
How can we build AI agents that reason, collaborate, use tools, and deliver reliable outcomes?

**Causal AI and decision intelligence**
Can we move beyond predicting what might happen and understand why it happens, what we can change, and what difference that change could make?

**Machine learning and new AI research**
How are new approaches changing model training, fine-tuning, evaluation, and the way we build intelligent systems?

**AI for business**
How can organizations use AI to improve decisions and outcomes? And how do we know whether the AI actually helped?

**Data quality and trustworthy AI**
Even the most advanced model can give misleading results when the underlying data is unreliable. How do we identify and address these problems?

## My Goals

I want to keep learning, experimenting, and sharing what I discover.

My goals are to:

- make interesting AI research easier to understand
- connect research with practical applications
- share reproducible experiments using public datasets
- encourage more critical thinking about AI systems

I also want this website to become a useful collection of articles, research notes, experiments, and ideas that others can learn from and build upon.

I'm not interested in explaining every new AI announcement. I would rather spend time understanding what is genuinely different, whether it works, and where it could be useful.

## How I Plan to Write

I'll keep the language simple and conversational, without losing the technical meaning.

Where possible, I'll start with an example, explain the idea, explore its practical value, and discuss its limitations.

I'll distinguish between what a research paper claims, what has been independently tested, and what I personally think.

I won't pretend every experiment worked or every interesting paper represents a breakthrough.

I'm learning too, and this website will reflect that.
